Field Manual // Chapter II

THE INFECTION CODEX

Ruleset governing GW9–GW14. Infection is a second, independent system layered on top of the survival rules above — it never replaces them.

01

Chapter II Introduction

CHAPTER II — THE INFECTION runs from GW9 to GW14: six gameweeks of the same survival format with one new variable — an infection that never goes away.

Every survivor enters the chapter with the hearts they finished Chapter I with. Nothing is reset and nothing is restored. Hearts, statuses and infection are tracked independently, and a single defeat can still end a tournament.

02

The Initial Infected

Four survivors enter Chapter II already infected. The roster is curated by the organizer and stays permanent for the whole chapter.

  • Wld Lhaj1 Heart
  • Abdellah Moghtanim1 Heart
  • Fatima1 Heart
  • Abdelghafour1 Heart
03

Infection Transmission

  • An infected player who wins infects their opponent.
  • An infected player who loses does not infect their opponent.
  • Infection spreads only through an infected player’s victory.
  • Infection is permanent throughout Chapter II.
  • Winning or recovering hearts does not cure infection.
04

Heart System

  • Players retain the hearts they had at the end of Chapter I.
  • Hearts are not reset at the beginning of Chapter II.
  • A DANGER player loses one heart upon defeat.
  • An INFECTED player loses one heart upon defeat.
  • If a player is both DANGER and INFECTED, they lose ONE heart only, never two.
  • Winning does not restore hearts.
One Heart Only

DANGER + INFECTED still costs one heart per defeat — never two.

05

Status & Infection

The two systems are independent and are tracked separately.

Status
  • SAFE
  • NORMAL
  • DANGER
Infection
  • INFECTED
  • UNINFECTED

A player can be SAFE + INFECTED, NORMAL + INFECTED, or DANGER + INFECTED. Infection does not automatically make a player DANGER.

06

Status Transitions

Chapter II uses exactly the same status transition rules as Chapter I.

07

Elimination

  • Reaching 0 hearts means permanent elimination.
  • Eliminated players cannot return.
  • No automatic protection exists for infected players or any status.
Zero Hearts

0 HEARTS = PERMANENT ELIMINATION.

08

No Recovery

  • No hearts are restored through victories.
  • Infection cannot be removed during Chapter II.
  • No automatic bonus hearts are awarded.

The Golden Rules

  1. An infected player who wins infects their opponent.
  2. An infected player who loses does not spread infection.
  3. An infected player loses one heart upon defeat.
  4. A DANGER player loses one heart upon defeat.
  5. DANGER + INFECTED still means one heart lost, not two.
  6. Winning never restores hearts.
  7. Infection remains permanent throughout Chapter II.
  8. Zero hearts means permanent elimination.
  9. Hearts are not reset at the beginning of Chapter II.
